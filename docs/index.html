Finly

A personal finance tracker that keeps every record on your own device.

Track what you spend

Log an expense in seconds against accounts and categories you define yourself.

Recurring rules

Set up rent, subscriptions and salary once, and the ledger keeps itself honest.

Budgets and insights

A monthly budget measured against real spending, and a breakdown of where it went.

Locked down

Optional database encryption and a biometric app lock, both switched on by you.

CSV in and out

Import your history, export it whenever you want. Your data stays portable.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. The app has no network code at all.

No ads. No trackers. No analytics. Uninstall the app and the data is gone — because there was never a copy anywhere else.
