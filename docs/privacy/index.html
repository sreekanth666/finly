Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

The short version: Finly collects nothing. It has no accounts, no servers and no network code. Everything you enter stays in a database on your own device, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the Finly Android application published by The C Studio. It describes how the app handles information, and it applies to everyone who installs it.

Information we collect

None. Finly does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information, financial information, device identifiers or usage data.

The app contains no network client, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting service. It does not use the Android Advertising ID. There is no account to create and no server for your information to be sent to, because no server exists.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is written to a local database on your device and never leaves it unless you deliberately export it. That includes:

  • Transactions, amounts, dates and notes you record
  • Payment alerts waiting for your review, if you use transaction detection
  • Accounts, categories and recurring rules you create
  • Budgets and currency preferences
  • App settings, including whether the app lock is switched on

This data is readable only by Finly, within the app's private storage area that Android isolates from other applications.

Optional database encryption

Finly offers optional at-rest encryption of its database, which you can turn on in Settings → Security. When you enable it, the app generates a 256-bit key using your device's secure random number generator and stores that key in Android's hardware-backed keystore. The key never leaves your device and is never transmitted or recoverable by us. If you do not enable encryption, the database is stored unencrypted inside the app's private storage.

Biometric app lock

You can optionally require a fingerprint, face unlock or your device passcode to open Finly. This check is performed entirely by Android. Finly receives only a yes-or-no result. It never receives, processes or stores your fingerprint, face data or passcode, and it holds no copy of any biometric information.

Optional transaction detection

Finly can offer to fill in expenses from the payment alerts your bank, card or UPI app sends you. It is off until you switch it on in Settings → Transaction detection, and it works in one of two ways:

  • Pasting a message. You copy an alert and paste it into Finly. Nothing else is read.
  • Notification access. If you grant it in Android's settings, Finly reads the notifications posted by the apps you choose — by default your SMS app and the banking, card and UPI apps you pick. Each notification is checked on the device; one that does not look like a payment alert is discarded without being stored. One that does is kept in the app's private storage so you can review it.

Every detected transaction waits in a review list until you confirm or dismiss it. When you confirm one, the original alert text is saved with the expense so you can see where it came from. Alert text is never uploaded, shared or sent anywhere; Finly still has no network code. Reviewed alerts are deleted after the retention period you choose, and switching the feature off stops all reading and clears anything waiting to be reviewed. You can withdraw notification access at any time in Android's settings.

Finly does not request permission to read your SMS inbox.

Exporting and importing your data

Finly can export your records as a CSV file and can import records from a CSV file you choose.

  • Exports are generated on your device. The file is then passed to the Android share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Once you send it to another app, cloud drive or contact, that destination's own privacy policy governs what happens to it.
  • Imports are read from the file you select and written into your local database. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

Permissions the app requests

  • Biometric / fingerprint — used only to power the optional app lock described above.
  • Notifications — used to show the optional daily reminder and, if you allow it, a count of transactions waiting for review. That count never shows amounts or payees.
  • Notification access — optional, granted by you in Android's settings, and used only for the transaction detection described above.

Children

Finly is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children — in fact, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, as described above.

Third parties

Finly includes no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking services. No third party receives information from the app.

Google Play may independently collect information about installs, crashes and device types as part of operating the store. That collection is governed by Google's privacy policy, not by this one, and we receive only aggregate, anonymous statistics through the Play Console.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual records inside the app at any time. To remove everything, uninstall Finly — this deletes the database and all of its contents from your device. Because no copy is held anywhere else, there is no server-side deletion request to make and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Your data is retained on your device for exactly as long as you keep it there.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights of access, correction, portability and erasure over personal data held by a company. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or erase. You exercise the practical equivalent directly: view and edit your records in the app, export them as CSV for portability, and uninstall to erase them.

Changes to this policy

If Finly ever adds a feature that changes how information is handled — for example an optional backup or sync service — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the date at the top of this page will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
